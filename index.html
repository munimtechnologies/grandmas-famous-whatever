Grandma's Famous Whatever

Grandma left you her famous recipe. She did not say what it is. She did not say how much of anything. She will taste it when you're done.

Controls: the mouse is your oven mitt. It's a bit slow, and a bit to the right. Click to do things. Keys if you must: Space pour, S shake, arrow keys stir and turn, Enter "Done, Grandma", M sound.

Follow her instructions. Or work out what she meant.

An entry for The WORST Game Jam (theme: Grandma's Instructions). No flashing lights. The sound is bad on purpose, but quiet. Takes about 2 minutes.
